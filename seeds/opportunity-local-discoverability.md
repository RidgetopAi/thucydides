# Thucydides Research Seed - Local Discoverability Sprint

## Mode
opportunity

## Subject
Research whether RidgetopAI can find underserved local-service businesses with visible online discoverability gaps, create specific evidence of improvement, run compliant personalized outreach, and convert the loop into the first capital path.

## Scope
Start with one service niche and one geography per batch. Focus on businesses where one closed customer lead can plausibly justify a $750-$1,500 local visibility sprint. Do not perform outreach from Thucydides. Thucydides researches and scores; Brian or the supervised operator loop approves any send.

Out of scope for the first batch:
- Regulated medical, financial, or legal claims.
- Fake reviews, review gating, ranking guarantees, or claims of Google affiliation.
- Bulk scraping or mass email.
- Full website builds before prospect interest is shown.

## Initial Angles
- Which local-service niches have high lead value and visibly uneven web/local profile quality?
- Which externally visible signals best predict a strong one-page mockup?
- Which public search/profile/PageSpeed signals can be captured repeatably?
- Which outreach claims are compliant, truthful, and specific?
- What minimum response signal should cause us to repeat, pivot, or kill the niche?

## Known Entry Points
- RidgetopAI RTA-037 sprint design.
- FTC CAN-SPAM guidance.
- Google Business Profile local ranking guidance.
- Google Search Central AI features guidance.
- Google PageSpeed Insights API.
- SBA small-business profiles.
- RidgetopAI opportunity target CSV template.

## First Shift Brief
Design the first 30-50 prospect target batch. Pick one candidate niche/geography only if the evidence supports it; otherwise open a thread for Brian's niche/geography decision. Produce entities for the niche, geography, offer, metrics, compliance rules, target scoring signals, and first experiment. Every entity must link to a source or operator observation.
