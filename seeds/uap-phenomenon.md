# Thucydides Research Seed - The UAP Phenomenon

## Subject
The history, people, programs, money, and institutions surrounding unidentified aerial phenomena — from the earliest documented military and government encounters through modern congressional disclosure efforts. This is not an advocacy project and it is not a debunking project. It is an investigative project. We follow threads, document what we find, note what we don't find, and let the evidence speak. If something is well-supported, we say so. If something falls apart under scrutiny, we say that too. Dead threads get documented — they may reconnect down the line.

Our investment: the people, the programs, the money trails, the institutional behavior. Either we are witnessing one of the most significant government disclosure processes in history, or we are witnessing one of the most sophisticated manipulation campaigns. Either way, the story of the people and institutions involved is real and worth mapping. The truth is in the details, the documents, the testimony, and the patterns.

## Scope
- **Time period**: 1940s (first documented military encounters) through present (2026), with earlier mentions if credible evidence surfaces
- **Geography**: Primarily US government and military, but including allied nations (Five Eyes, NATO) where programs intersect
- **In scope**: People and their roles, government programs (official and alleged), funding and appropriations, military encounters with documentation, congressional action and legislation, whistleblower testimony and its corroboration, intelligence community involvement, defense contractor connections, institutional behavior patterns, documents (declassified, leaked, FOIA), the "disclosure" narrative itself — who is pushing it, why, and with what evidence
- **Out of scope**: Propulsion physics speculation, ancient aliens theories, abduction narratives (unless directly connected to documented programs), social media UFO culture, entertainment media influence. We are not here to explain *what* UAPs are — we are here to map *what happened, who was involved, and what the evidence actually supports*

## Initial Angles
1. **The beginning** — What are the earliest documented military/government encounters? Kenneth Arnold, Roswell, the 1946 ghost rockets? What was the government's initial institutional response?
2. **The official programs** — Project Sign (1948), Project Grudge (1949), Project Blue Book (1952-1969). What did they actually conclude? Who ran them? What happened to the data?
3. **The Robertson Panel (1953)** — Did the CIA recommend institutional suppression of reporting? What were the actual documents versus the narrative about them?
4. **J. Allen Hynek's arc** — From Blue Book skeptic to serious researcher. What changed his mind? What did he actually see in the data?
5. **The gap years (1969-2007)** — Blue Book closes, officially "nothing happens" for 38 years. What was actually going on? Any programs, any institutional interest?
6. **The money trail** — Senator Harry Reid, $22M DIA funding, AAWSAP/AATIP. Who proposed it, who approved it, where did the money go? Bigelow Aerospace connection.
7. **The modern military encounters** — USS Nimitz (2004), USS Roosevelt (2014-2015). Multiple sensors, multiple witnesses. What is actually documented vs. what is narrative?
8. **The 2017 break** — NY Times story reveals AATIP. Who leaked? Why? What was the strategy?
9. **The whistleblowers** — David Grusch, Luis Elizondo, others. DOPSR clearance, Inspector General complaints, congressional testimony under oath. What is legally verifiable vs. what is claim?
10. **Congressional action** — UAP Task Force, AARO, the Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Act, hearings. Who is pushing legislation and why? What got stripped and by whom?
11. **The institutional resistance question** — Who opposes disclosure and why? Defense contractors? Classification authorities? Genuine national security? Embarrassment?
12. **The "legacy programs" claim** — Grusch's central allegation of crash retrieval and reverse engineering programs. What corroborates it? What contradicts it? Who else has made similar claims and with what evidence?

## RESEARCH PRINCIPLES — READ THESE FIRST

This research follows strict investigative principles:

1. **Follow evidence, not narratives.** We don't start with a conclusion and find evidence for it. We start with threads and follow them wherever they lead.

2. **Document everything.** If a thread leads nowhere, that's a finding. If a source contradicts our working picture, record it. Dead ends are data.

3. **No dismissal, no advocacy.** We don't dismiss claims because they sound extraordinary. We don't advocate for claims because they're exciting. We assess evidence quality and document what we find.

4. **The "official narrative" gets the same scrutiny as the "alternative narrative."** AARO's "no evidence" claim is a claim that requires supporting evidence, just as Grusch's allegations require supporting evidence. Both get Jester review.

5. **People and institutions are the anchor.** We can verify who held what position, what programs existed, what money was appropriated, what testimony was given under oath. Start from what's verifiable and work outward.

6. **Confidence levels must be honest.** A claim made under oath with DOPSR clearance is different from an anonymous Reddit post. Both get recorded. Confidence levels reflect the evidence, not our hopes.

7. **Threads may reconnect.** A dead thread in Shift 3 might suddenly matter in Shift 15 when a new name appears. Never delete threads — resolve or leave them open.

8. **Follow the money.** Appropriations, contracts, corporate connections, lobbying. The money trail is often the most verifiable thread.

**For this topic especially**: Jester should challenge BOTH directions:
- Challenge "nothing to see here" claims — what evidence contradicts official dismissals?
- Challenge "smoking gun" claims — what evidence is actually thin or single-source?
- Challenge institutional narratives — why did organizations act the way they did?
- Challenge whistleblower claims — is there corroboration beyond the person's own statements?

### How Jester Results Are Handled
**Jester is a challenger, not a judge.** Jester's voice has equal weight to research agents — not more, not less.

When Jester returns assessments:
- **"accept"** → Record the claim at current confidence. Jester found it solid.
- **"qualify"** → Add nuance to the description. The core claim stands but needs context.
- **"dispute"** → **THIS IS A BRANCH, NOT AN OVERRIDE.** Record the original claim AND the challenge. Set `challenged = TRUE`, `challenge_outcome = 'disputed'`, `dispute_status = 'open'`, and `dispute_note` with both sides. Open a thread for future shifts to investigate BOTH sides.
- **"investigate"** → Not enough evidence either way. Open a thread. Don't record as fact yet.

### When to Deploy Judge
- When Jester and a research agent directly contradict each other AND both have evidence
- When two research agents report conflicting facts
- When a disputed claim has been investigated from both sides and needs resolution

**The Judge is rare.** Most disputes should stay open and accumulate evidence across shifts.

### RELATIONSHIPS ARE CRITICAL
Entities without relationships are isolated nodes. Every shift should insert relationships. For each entity, consider:
- **employed-by** / **managed** — Who worked where? Who led whom?
- **founded** / **co-founded** — Who created what organization?
- **directed** / **oversaw** — Who ran what program?
- **funded-by** / **contracted-with** / **appropriated** — Who paid for what?
- **testified** / **reported-to** — Who told whom what, officially?
- **mentored** / **influenced** — Intellectual and professional lineages
- **collaborated** / **rivaled** / **conflicted-with** — Working relationships
- **preceded** / **succeeded** — Program and organizational genealogy
- **corroborated** / **contradicted** — Evidentiary relationships
- **classified** / **declassified** — Information control relationships
- **lobbied** / **opposed** — Political relationships

### ENTITY-SOURCE LINKAGES ARE CRITICAL — DO NOT SKIP
**Every entity must be linked to the sources that support it.** The `entity_sources` table connects entities to their evidence with specific claim text. This is the provenance chain — without it, no entity can be traced back to its evidence.

For every entity you insert or update this shift, link it to its supporting source(s):
```sql
INSERT INTO entity_sources (entity_id, source_id, claim)
VALUES (
  (SELECT id FROM entities WHERE name = 'Harry Reid' AND topic = 'uap-phenomenon'),
  (SELECT id FROM sources WHERE title = 'Senator Reid SAP Request - DIA FOIA' AND topic = 'uap-phenomenon'),
  'Reid wrote June 2009 letter to Deputy SecDef Lynn requesting SAP status, citing highly sensitive unconventional aerospace-related findings'
) ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING;
```

**Rules for entity-source linkages:**
- Every new entity MUST have at least one source linked to it
- The `claim` field should contain the SPECIFIC factual claim the source supports — not a vague summary
- Good claim: `"Condon fired Saunders in February 1968 for allegedly leaking the Low memo"`
- Bad claim: `"Information about Saunders"`
- If an entity has multiple sources, insert one row per source with the specific claim each source supports
- When updating an existing entity with new information from a new source, add a new entity_sources row

**Parse agent CLAIM lines into entity_sources rows.** Agents return structured CLAIM lines like:
```
CLAIM|Entity Name|Source Title|Specific claim text
```
Each of these maps directly to an `entity_sources` INSERT.

## DECISION FRAMEWORK

### When to Deploy Jester
- Any claim with confidence > 0.7 from a single source
- Any claim that would significantly change the narrative
- Any claim about motivations or causation (not just facts)
- Anything that seems "too clean" or "too convenient"
- **Any official/institutional narrative that hasn't been questioned** — official sources have backstories too
- **Any whistleblower claim that lacks independent corroboration**
- **Any "everyone knows" assumption** — these are often the least examined

### When to Deploy Judge
- Jester and an agent have direct contradicting evidence (not just different interpretation)
- Two agents report directly conflicting facts
- A disputed claim has accumulated evidence from both sides across multiple shifts

### Confidence Calibration
- 0.9-1.0: Multiple independent primary sources, direct evidence, survived Jester challenge. Testimony under oath with corroboration. Official documents with independent verification.
- 0.7-0.8: Good secondary sources, well-corroborated, qualified by Jester. Single-source testimony under oath. Official documents without independent verification.
- 0.5-0.6: Single credible source, plausible but unverified. DOPSR-cleared claims without independent corroboration. Credible journalist reports without primary documents.
- 0.3-0.4: Speculative, based on inference, or from low-reliability sources. Single anonymous source claims. Logical inference from patterns.
- 0.1-0.2: Rumor or unverified claim, recorded for investigation only. Social media claims. Unsourced allegations.

## SHIFT STRATEGY

### Shift 1: The Beginning (FOUNDATIONS)
- Establish 1940s entities: people, organizations, events
- Map the "official narrative" of early encounters and initial government response
- Create foundational timeline entities (events with dates)
- Open threads for everything needing deeper investigation
- Focus on BREADTH — we need the skeleton before the detail

### Shifts 2+: Follow the Plan
- Consult the Shift Planning Framework above for your phase
- But ALWAYS check open threads and disputed claims from previous shifts first
- If a previous shift flagged something as "critical priority," address it

### Any Shift: Important Principles
- **Don't rush.** There are more shifts after you (check your count). Do thorough work.
- **Relationships matter as much as entities.** A person without connections is a dead node.
- **Disputed claims are valuable.** They show where the interesting questions are.
- **Cross-topic awareness.** If you discover an entity that might connect to another research topic (transistor history, Cold War programs, etc.), note it explicitly.
- **Leave clear handoffs.** The next shift should know exactly where you left off and what to do next.
- **Track your shift budget.** If you're on Shift 12 and haven't started the modern era yet, adjust.
